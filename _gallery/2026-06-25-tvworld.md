My submission to [3D Modelling Jam #12!](https://itch.io/jam/3d-modelling-jam-12) (SOMEHOW made in 26 hours...)
The trailer release of Persona4 Revival made me want to revisit the original game's aesthetic. Prominently featuring TV as its main theme, I want to evoke a mysterious and overwhelming feel from the avalanche of TVs scattered around that the game likes to feature.

Modeled in Blender<br>
Post Processing in IbisPaintX
