## What Should Be Done If I Died
I'll update this section as I progress through and get better bearings on my life and have more things to add and things I'd like to preserve.

The Do's and Dont's are as follows:
- Don't do any traditional funerary procession.
  I don't want a three nights nor Night 40 procession, it'll only burden those I've left behind. Unless someone else is willing to carry the expenses.
- Don't cry at my funeral
  I'd hate to see anyone cry.
- Do laugh at my funeral
  The last thing I'd like to see from my beloveds are their happy faces.
