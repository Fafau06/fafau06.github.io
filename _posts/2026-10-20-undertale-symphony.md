On Sunday, October 11th, the UNDERTALE: Determination Symphony was held at Ciputra Artpreneur in Jakarta. It marked the first official UNDERTALE-related event in Indonesia. And since I had enough time on my hands, I went there and... did not watch the symphony, at all. 
<!--more-->

Mind you, it's not that I didn't want to watch the symphony because deep down in my heart; I know that I want to go, I know that I want to be in the auditorium, and I know for certain that I want to experience this (possibly) once-in-a-lifetime event of the symphony’s world tour.

But when the tickets were first announced on sale back in the month of June, do you know how it sold out in under a week?? and how they have to add a matinée show to accommodate the overflowing demands?? and how penniless I was that I didn't even bother getting myself a ticket?!?!

So, could you blame me for backing down?

With at least *that* much optimism, it's not hard to imagine that I kept my expectations low and already accepted myself not going. Until someone in the Undertale Deltarune Indonesia discord server started mentioning that it's a good opportunity to hold an offline gathering, and how this once-in-a-lifetime event could bring together so many other Undertale fans from across the country in one massive auditorium. Well, color me excited!

I quickly turned around from moping around not being able to watch the symphony to being excited at the prospect of meeting other people from the community! That's not something you can just arrange on any Tuesday afterall. So I helped around with the preparations for this gathering, minimal as it was, I still want to feel like I was contributing something to this community I hold dear.

So, while the other discord server admins were preparing the major marketing campaign. I helped out by making a new logo for the community (wonderfully designed and sketched out by [Sabita Onitsuga](https://sabitaonitsuga.carrd.co/)) and buying accessories as needed for the gathering. I also made a little something for myself too, that being some limited amount of pins that I gave out privately during the gathering.

![myprints](/assets/webp/myprints.webp)
<small>I bought and printed some ID Cards and pins!</small>

Then it was announced that my community would be giving away some freebies. I wasn't involved in the making so I couldn't tell much about the process, but the freebies giveaway was the major campaign that the other admins were working on. They were planning on giving those away during the symphony and assembled a field team, but there was an issue, not many admins would be around or available for the symphony — and the closest admin for the symphony… turned out to be me.

Yes, me, the same me who was dreading not going for not having a ticket now has an actual solid reason to go there! Even if it's only for the gathering and giving away freebies, at least I’ll still be there!

![freebies](/assets/webp/freebies.webp)
<small>The freebies that we gave out. So sorry if you missed out on these! (Wish I could take the physical photo but I only have one of them hhh)</small>

That said, meeting and talking to people wasn't my strongest suit so I was still dreading that part even though I was undeniably excited...

They eventually formed a field team that consisted of me and two other people. But first, I had to do a preliminary survey of the Artpreneur.

So about a week before the event. I went there, only to find out that it was empty and absolutely eerie. Apparently they're still closed and didn't have anything on (even the lights and the escalators) during days where there are no shows. But it was so bad I started having a panic attack and had to call some people to accompany me.

It didn't end there! I was then questioned by some security guards who thought I was going to rent the place and needed an auditorium?? Luckily, I sorted the issue out somehow but in return I got the namecard of the sales manager...? So that was something...

Remember how I said earlier that I designed a brand new logo and made my own freebies? I did all those things in a one-week-crunch away from the actual event... yeah, it was quite the busy week (which also explained why I didn't blog anything during that week!)

But the long awaited day finally came! I was two hours late from the agreed “four hours before the matinée show” but turns out even that is still pretty early. I quickly met up with the other field team members who have travelled pretty far to get here and we started handing out the freebies.

Now, I'm definitely the more quiet one and I didn't remember doing much for the six hours I was there… I wish I could explain more about the events but I'm such a private and secluded person that I don't remember interacting with much of the people there… sorry ;,,

Despite that though, I wasn't expecting that so many others were going to give out freebies too? At first I thought that only our community was going to give out freebies, but apparently so many individuals have also prepared their own freebies to give and trade with each other! It was quite the sight to see the freebies-giver, surrounding every new person who just entered the room, just to give them a freebie.

![myfreebies](/assets/webp/myfreebies.webp)
<small>These are all the freebies I got from everyone that day!</small>

Few things that stood out the most to me was the number of cosplayers present, even the other field team members were in cosplay too! I never was one for cosplaying so I didn't come up with anything. Yet, somehow, someone thought that my get-up for the day was a cosplay and they even tried to guess which character I was…

But honestly, seeing other people's abilities to come up with variations on the same simple pixel sprites were inspiring. Each cosplayer put their own spin on the design and made their cosplay memorable and distinctive. Even amongst the sea of Chara and Kris cosplayers out there, you could still make out the small little differences that each of them brings to their own costume.

There were many who cosplayed various other Undertale related characters, even all the AUs stuff! …even Hatsune Miku?? And some cosplayers definitely have spent more time and money into their costumes than I could possibly comprehend, but their labor of love was properly displayed with pride for the entire symphony-goers to see!

I stayed quite long though, enough to greet both the matinée and night show goers, but I eventually went home when the night show was on since… I have no business overstaying there and the *actual* staff members were already kicking us non-goers out.

At the end of the day, it really wasn't a stretch to say that I came there not to enjoy the symphony, but rather something else. It was to see the community, all the people that enjoyed this wonderful game and the works of Toby Fox in person, to know that other UNDERTALE fans existed beyond the screen. Even though I didn't interact much with everyone, it was still such a joy to see and to meet the wider fanbase out there in person. 

A massive thank you to everyone who came and gave away all sorts of trinkets and freebies! And especially to my fellow members of the field team: [Faquith](https://www.instagram.com/faquith) and [Dis](https://www.instagram.com/disthedrawer) for running and managing the social media promotions and having to come to Jakarta and helping out. And the other admins from Undertale Deltarune Indonesia who have helped out along the way!

Hope I can see everyone again sometime!

… If I can go, that is… :ralsei_splat:
